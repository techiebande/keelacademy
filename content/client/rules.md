# The Lantern Home support rulebook

These are the rules the support team applies when a customer writes in with a problem. Rosa Delgado keeps this document. When two rules seem to disagree, the later rule in this list wins, and the case goes to Rosa.

## Time limits

1. A customer can report a problem with an order up to 14 days after the delivery date in the courier's record. Count the delivery date as day 0.
2. If there is no courier record for the order, or the record has no delivery date, the 14 days have not started. Treat the case as "not yet delivered".
3. A report that arrives after the 14 days is refused, unless the item is faulty in a way the customer could not have seen when it arrived (for example, a kettle that stops working in week three). Faulty items are covered for 6 months. Those cases go to Rosa.

## Parcels that did not arrive

4. If the courier's record says the parcel was delivered and the customer says nothing arrived, ask the customer to check with neighbours and at the building's reception, and wait 2 working days.
5. If after 2 working days the customer still has nothing, refund the order or resend it, whichever the customer prefers.
6. If the courier's record shows no delivery and it has been more than 7 days since the order was dispatched, resend the order at once, unless the customer asks for a refund instead.

## Damaged items

7. For a damaged item, the customer must send a photo of the damage before any refund is paid. If the message has no photo, reply asking for one. The 14 days pause while we wait.
8. Once a photo shows the damage, refund the item's price or resend it, whichever the customer prefers. The customer does not have to send the damaged item back.
9. If the photo is unclear, or the damage looks like it happened after delivery, the case goes to Rosa.

## Wrong item delivered

10. If the customer received a different item from the one on the order, resend the correct item at no charge.
11. If the wrong item's price is under 50.00, the customer keeps it. If it is 50.00 or more, arrange a courier collection before the correct item is sent.

## Changes of mind

12. A customer who simply no longer wants an item can return it within the 14 days, in its original packing, and is refunded the item's price once it arrives back at the warehouse. Delivery charges are not refunded.
13. Items marked "final sale" on the order cannot be returned for a change of mind. They can still be refunded if damaged or faulty.

## Money

14. A refund is paid the same way the customer paid. Card payments go back to the card; bank transfers go back to the bank account.
15. A support agent can approve a refund of less than 200.00 alone. A refund of 200.00 or more needs Rosa's approval before it is paid.
16. Never refund more than the price of the item on the order, plus the delivery charge if the whole order is being refunded.
17. A refund is paid once per order line. If a refund for that line already exists in the record, the case goes to Rosa.

## Records

18. Every decision is written down with: the message number, the order number, the decision (refund, resend, ask for more, refuse, or passed to Rosa), the amount if money is involved, the name of the person who decided, and the date and time.
19. A record is never edited. If a decision was wrong, a new record is written that refers to the old one and corrects it.

## When in doubt

20. If the customer's message does not match the records, if the rules above do not clearly cover the case, or if the agent is unsure for any reason, the case goes to Rosa. Nothing is paid until she decides.
