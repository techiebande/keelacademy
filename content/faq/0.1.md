# Unit 0.1 unstuck notes

## Why does my first section fail when I name a tool? {#name-the-problem-not-the-tool}

A tool is a fix. The problem section is not the place for a fix. If you name a tool there, the reader learns what you want to buy, not what breaks at OmniCart today.

Check your sentences for any word that says how the work should get done. Then replace it with what happens now, with numbers.

Before: OmniCart needs a faster system to sort its returns.

After: OmniCart handles about 4,000 returns, claims, slips, and disputes each month. Each one waits 2 to 3 days before anyone reviews it.

The after version can be checked against the dock. The before version cannot. You cannot fix what you cannot describe, so describe first.

## My three leaders all seem to want speed. How do I make them differ? {#three-leaders-sound-the-same}

Speed is what Sarah Jenkins, the VP of Operations, wants. The other two leaders want something else. If all three lines say faster, you have written one leader three times.

Ask each leader what would make them agree, even if the wait stayed the same.

Before: The CFO wants returns handled faster so refunds go out sooner.

After: The CFO wants every refund tracked in whole cents, such as 4520 cents, in a record nobody can change.

For the Trust and Safety Officer, write about rules and proof. She wants every return inside the 14 day window and every damage claim backed by a photo.

## My today steps skip the four papers. What am I missing? {#steps-skip-the-four-papers}

A clerk decides a case by reading four papers. Your steps must name them: the order receipt, the delivery slip, the unboxing photo, and the return policy. The words files, records, or documents are not enough.

Walk through CLM-20841 in your head. Where does the clerk find each paper? Write that down as a step.

Before: 3. The clerk checks the files and decides.

After: 3. The clerk opens the order receipt for ORD-8821 and the delivery slip. Then she finds the unboxing photo and reads the return policy.

Keep 4 to 7 steps in total. Start when the box arrives at the dock. End at the refund paid or denied.

## My target steps have no number or no person. What does the check want? {#target-needs-a-number-and-a-person}

The target section needs three things. A wait of under 1 hour. A person who reviews the hard cases. At least one amount written in whole cents.

Look at each step and ask two questions. Can I measure it? Is anyone in it?

Before: 5. Hard cases get handled quickly and correctly.

After: 5. A hard case, such as a claim with no photo or a return sent back late, goes to a reviewer. The reviewer is a person. The reviewer decides within 1 hour.

Quickly is not a number. Handled is not a person. Replace each vague word with a number or a name.
