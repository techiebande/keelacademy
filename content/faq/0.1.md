# Unit 0.1 unstuck notes

## How do I fix forbidden technology words? {#forbidden-technology-words}

We describe what happens, not what tool does it.
The grader stops if you name tools or build methods.
Use ordinary human roles instead.
Write clerk or reviewer rather than technical terms.

Before: Our automated system routes claims to a support agent.

After: A clerk routes claims to a specialist for review.

## What if my problem statement has four sentences? {#too-many-sentences}

The first section must stay short and direct.
The rubric allows at most three sentences.
Put all the core facts together.
Name the monthly count and the wait time in those three sentences.

Before: OmniCart gets many returns. Packages arrive daily at the dock. The team receives about 4,000 cases each month. Each case sits for 2 to 3 days before review.

After: OmniCart receives about 4,000 cases each month. Each case waits 2 to 3 days before review begins. Customers grow upset while claims sit untouched.

## Why must I write 4520 cents instead of 45 dollars? {#dollar-amounts-instead-of-cents}

Our finance team tracks every single cent.
Decimals cause rounding errors across thousands of orders.
Whole integer numbers keep every ledger line exact.
Write the full cent amount as a whole number without decimals or symbols.

Before: The clerk approves a refund of $45.20 for the damaged item.

After: The clerk approves a refund of 4520 cents for the damaged item.

## How do I name all four documents in the current steps? {#missing-required-documents}

A clerk checks four documents before paying a claim.
Missing even one document causes the rubric check to fail.
Make sure your steps mention each document by its full name.
The four documents are the order receipt, the delivery slip, the unboxing photo, and the return policy.

Before: A clerk checks the paperwork and photos before sending payment.

After: A clerk matches the order receipt to the delivery slip. The clerk then inspects the unboxing photo against the return policy.
