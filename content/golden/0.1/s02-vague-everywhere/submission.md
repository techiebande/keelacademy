# Lantern Home: client brief

## The problem

Lantern Home is a growing online retailer that wants to improve its customer experience. Customer service is slow and could be much better. Customers are unhappy and the company wants to change that.

## Who cares, and what done means to each of them

Sarah Johnson is the owner and wants the business to succeed and customers to be happy. Michael Brown is the accountant and wants things to be done properly. Rachel Smith runs the support team and wants the team to do a good job.

## How a message is handled today

A customer writes in and eventually an agent reads the message and deals with it. This can take a while.

## How a message should be handled

Messages should be dealt with quickly and correctly so that customers are satisfied and the business runs smoothly.
