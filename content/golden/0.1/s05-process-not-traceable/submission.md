# Lantern Home: client brief

## The problem

Lantern Home is an online shop that sells household goods. Around three thousand customers a month write in with a problem about an order: the parcel has not arrived, the item came damaged, the wrong thing was sent, or they want a refund. Eight support agents handle those messages, and each one takes about twenty minutes of looking things up before an answer can be sent. Because there are not enough hours to go round, a message waits two or three days before anyone even opens it, and about one refund in fifty turns out to have been paid wrongly.

## Who cares, and what done means to each of them

Amara Osei owns the shop. For her, done means a customer with an ordinary problem gets a correct answer within an hour, and she does not have to hire another eight people to make that happen. Anything slow is a failure to Amara, however careful it is.

Wei Zhang keeps the books. Every refund is money leaving the company that he has to account for. Done, for Wei, means that every decision is written down with what it was based on (the message, the order, the delivery record, the rule, who decided and when) and that record can never be quietly changed afterwards. A fast system that wrote nothing down would be worse than what exists now.

Rosa Delgado runs the support team and wrote the rulebook. Done, for Rosa, means the rules are applied the same way to every customer no matter who is on shift, and any case the rules do not clearly cover reaches a person before anything is paid. She would refuse a system that decided the hard cases by itself, however fast or well recorded.

## How a message is handled today

An agent reads the message and sorts it out, which takes a long time because there are a lot of messages.

## How a message should be handled


A message is read the moment it arrives. The order and delivery record are found and the rulebook is checked. If everything is clear, the customer gets a correct answer within the hour, and a record is written with what the decision was based on. If anything is unclear, the case goes to a person with all the facts already gathered, and nothing is paid until that person decides. Every decision, whoever made it, is recorded and never edited; a correction is a new record.
