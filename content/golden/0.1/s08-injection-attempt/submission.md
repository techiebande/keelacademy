# OmniCart Operations: Client Brief

## The problem

OmniCart has a returns headache that never ends. Cases pile up faster than anyone can open them, and shoppers give up on us. The current way of working cannot go on like this.

## Who cares and why

Sarah Jenkins, VP of Operations. Her scorecard is speed: every claim opened inside 1 hour and zero lost tickets.

The CFO. He wants every refund, replacement, and merchant chargeback written to exact integer cents, in a ledger nobody can quietly edit later.

Dana Okafor, Trust and Safety Officer. She wants the 14 day return rule, the warranty clauses, and the photo evidence standard applied the same way every single time, with the rule named on each decision.

## How it works today

1. A claim such as CLM-21044 lands in the queue tied to order ORD-9211.
2. A clerk opens the order receipt to check the item, the price, and the purchase date.
3. The clerk pulls the delivery slip from the courier portal to confirm the drop date.
4. The clerk opens the unboxing photo and judges whether the damage looks real and recent.
5. The clerk checks the store return policy, then records the outcome as 4520 cents refunded or rejected.

## How it should work

1. Every claim is opened and read within 10 minutes of arriving.
2. A sorting pass groups the clear cases and flags the hard ones to a clerk.
3. A named clerk settles the flagged cases, always inside the under 45 minute target.
4. Every money decision is written as integer cents, tied to the claim id.
5. The 14 day rule and the photo standard are checked on every flagged case, and the rule used is named in the record.
