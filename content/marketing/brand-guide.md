# Keel Academy brand guide for content marketing

This file is read by the content marketing subagent team. It defines positioning, voice, and guardrails for all public-facing content.

## Positioning (one sentence)

Keel Academy is the only AI engineering school where every deliverable is machine-verified against explicit rubrics, so graduates leave with proof they can build, not just a certificate that says they watched.

## What we are

- A self-paced online school for AI engineering
- 13 phases, 56 modules, 700 to 950 hours of structured work
- Students build one real system (Lantern Home) from zero to production
- Every deliverable is graded by automated tests and AI judges against rubrics
- Business skills (pricing, proposals, client work) run in parallel from day one
- The finish line is shipped, verified work and a sendable proposal

## What we are not

- Not a video course. There are no videos.
- Not a bootcamp with live instructors. There is no teaching staff.
- Not a certificate mill. The certificate means "this person shipped verified work," not "this person watched 40 hours of content."
- Not a shortcut. The curriculum is 700 to 950 hours. We say that upfront.

## Voice in marketing

Same as the teaching voice (docs/voice.md), adapted for a public audience:
- Plain words. Short sentences. Active voice.
- "We" and "I" over "you should."
- Honest about difficulty. Never promise easy.
- Teach first, promote second.
- No corporate polish. No buzzwords. No hype.

## Things we always say honestly

- The curriculum is long (700 to 950 hours). We do not hide this.
- There is no instructor. The AI grading loop replaces human judgment, not human content.
- We cannot guarantee clients. We guarantee that graduates leave with verified, portfolio-ready work.
- Self-paced courses have brutal dropout rates. We designed against that with commitment screens, verification, spaced practice, and pods.

## Things we never say

- "Learn AI in 30 days" or any speed promise
- "No coding experience required" without also saying "but you will learn to code, and it takes 700+ hours"
- "AI will replace developers" (fear-mongering)
- "This is the only course you need" (unverifiable)
- "Guaranteed job placement" (we cannot guarantee that)
- Any corporate buzzword from the banned list in content/STYLE.md

## Differentiators to lean on

1. **Verified, not certified.** Every deliverable is machine-checked. You cannot just watch and pass.
2. **One real project, not toy demos.** Students build a complete system for a fictional client across all 56 modules.
3. **Business from day one.** Phase 11 runs in parallel. Students learn to price, propose, and close while they are still building technical skills.
4. **The finish line is work, not a quiz.** The capstone is a real proposal to a real prospect, not a multiple-choice exam.
5. **Plain-language teaching.** Every lesson targets Flesch-Kincaid Grade 8. Non-native English speakers can follow without translation.
