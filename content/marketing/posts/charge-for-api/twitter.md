If your AI side project only cost $2 in API credits, do not be surprised when clients refuse to pay $2,000 for it.

The technical skills to build a simple AI wrapper are common now. That makes them cheap. 

Here is how you actually build sellable AI systems.

2/

A business does not care about your technology. A regional operations director does not want an AI agent. 

They want to process 4000 merchant payout disputes a month without hiring five more people. Toys do not get budget approval. Solutions do.

3/

Step 1: Find the expensive workflow.

Do not start with the model. Start with the workflow. Look for processes where people spend hours reading, routing, or extracting information from messy documents. 

Focus on the back office, not the creative department.

4/

Step 2: Scope the real requirements.

The workflow involves messy PDFs, outdated databases, and contradictory policy documents. You have to map every edge case. 

If you do not ask these questions, you will build a system that breaks on day one.

5/

Step 3: Price based on value, not hours.

Charge based on the value you create. If your system saves the company 100 hours a month, calculate what those hours cost. 

This requires a difficult conversation about money. Do not skip it.

6/

Learning to write code and learning to sell cannot happen in sequence. You have to build the business muscle while you build the technical muscle.
